# Budget promises: can ESV data answer them?

Findings of the spike `hemsida-9ce.1`, 2026-11-23. It decides how `hemsida-dl5.4` (the 170 unassessed `type: budget` promises of 2022-2026) can proceed. Inputs: `src/data/budget/expenditure.json` (ESV årsutfall 2006-2025, release 2025, see `docs/budget-data.md`), `macro.json` (SCB KPI and BNP), the promise files, and the verdict rules and evidence tiers in `docs/data-model.md` ("Budget evidence", "Evidence figures and the three tiers"). No data or verdicts are changed by this spike.

The machine-readable classification of all 170 promises is `docs/budget-promises-classification.json` (id, class, anslag codes, one-line reason).

## Answer in short

1. **ESV anslag data answers a promise only when the promise is about the size of one nameable anslag.** That is 46 of 170 (plus 7 that also need an SCB denominator). Most of the rest are earmarks inside a larger anslag, headcounts, or rate promises.
2. **(Superseded by `hemsida-dl5.6`: tiered budget evidence now qualifies, see `docs/data-model.md`, "Budget verdict rule".) No ESV evidence could qualify a verdict when this spike was written.** `checkVerdictEvidence` ignores `budget` evidence by design: `fulfilled` and `partially_fulfilled` need an `sfs` entry, `in_progress` needs `dir`, `sou`, `ds` or `prop`. The data model says budget promises "wait for their own evidence tiers (ESV outcome data)", but those tiers have no verdict rule yet. So dl5.4 cannot set `fulfilled`/`unfulfilled` for budget promises until a decision is made on a budget verdict rule (bead filed, see "Recommendation"). The data is good enough to support that rule for the 46 anslag promises.
3. **Settled conventions** for comparing an anslag: baseline = outcome (`utfall`) of 2022, the last full year before the term's first budget (2023); end = 2025, the latest ESV year; report nominal and real (KPI to 2025 prices) side by side; only the nominal change decides a verdict; the real figure is context (decision `hemsida-dl5.6`, rule in `docs/data-model.md`, "Budget verdict rule").
4. **Opposition promises (C, MP, S, V; 69 of the 170)** have no budget to execute. Decision (`hemsida-dl5.6`): they are judged only on that party's own acts (motion, own budget alternative, vote against its own promise); otherwise `not_assessable`. They are never measured against the government's outcome.

Lessons:

- **Baseline.** The 2022 outcome is the right default because 2023 is the first Tidö budget, but one-offs distort it (pandemic and elpris money in 2022: `0901006` Bidrag till folkhälsa och sjukvård falls 32 000 to 10 798 as covid funds end). The evidence `note` must state the baseline when it is not 2022, and anslag with one-offs may use 2021 or the budget ceiling instead.
- **Nominal vs real.** Nominal is the right default on the budget pages (it is what the Riksdag votes), but for "öka" the real series changes the answer: `2101003` Energiforskning +8.2 % nominal is -3.7 % real; `2501001` Kommunalekonomisk utjämning +10.3 % nominal is -1.9 % real; `1713003` friluftsorganisationer +7.2 % is -4.7 % real. Decision (`hemsida-dl5.6`): the verdict rests on the nominal figure only, because that is what the Riksdag voted. The real figure is shown as context next to it and can say that a nominal rise is a cut in purchasing power, but it never qualifies or blocks a verdict.
- **Years.** Outcome is only available to 2025. The 2026 ceiling is in BP 2026 (regeringen xlsx, deferred in `docs/budget-data.md`); it can only be stated intention or ceiling, never outcome. A term-close verdict for 2022-2026 rests on 2023-2025 outcome plus the 2026 ceiling.
- **Ceiling vs outcome.** The stored `budget` evidence has `amounts.before/after` but no field saying whether they are the ceiling (`budget` plus `andrad`) or the `utfall`. That makes the tier ambiguous and was added as `tier` and `baselineNote` in `hemsida-dl5.6`.
- **Anchoring.** An anslag name never shows whose decision it was. Every budget verdict needs a `prop`/`bet` entry on the promise that introduces or changes the money, so the verdict says what was decided and the anslag shows what was spent.
- **Several anslag per promise.** 18 of the 46 answerable promises name two or more anslag (rail plus road, rättskedjan, UO sums). The `budget` evidence is one anslag per entry, so these use several entries, or one entry per anslag with `relevance: partial`.

Answerable anslag by area (class `anslag`): transport infrastructure (road and rail, 11 promises, `2201001`/`2201002`/`2201007`), energy, laddinfrastruktur and housing investment support (11, `2101002`/`2101003`/`2101008`/`2101012`/`2001018`/`1801007`/`1801008`), rättsväsendet and Tullverket (5, `0401001`-`0401006`, `0301002`), bistånd and EU fee (4, `0701001`, `2701001`), civil protection and klimatanpassning (4, `0602002`/`0602004`), health (3, `0901008`/`0901011`/`0902006`), kommun grants (2, `2501001`), other (6: research, friluftsorganisationer, havsmiljö, utsatta skolor, förorenade områden, omställningsstöd). The `anslag_macro` class is defence 2 % of BNP (4 promises), aid 1 % of BNI (2) and the KD deficit target (1).

Classification confidence: the `anslag` and `rate_law` classes are high confidence. `prop_text` vs `not_answerable` is a judgement on whether a prop could state an amount; the dl5.4 child for that class re-checks it with the prop text.

## What an answerable promise looks like (illustrative, not set)

For a promise in class `anslag` the evidence would be one `budget` entry per anslag plus the anchoring `prop`/`bet`, and the reasoning would state both amounts and both changes, for example: "Anslaget 1:6 Kriminalvården hade ett utfall på 11 939,5 mn kr 2022 och 20 281,9 mn kr 2025, en ökning med 69,9 procent i löpande priser och 51,1 procent i fasta priser." This is data, not a verdict; whether it supports `fulfilled`, `partially_fulfilled` or only `in_progress` depends on the rule the first child bead decides.

## Recommendation for `hemsida-dl5.4`

dl5.4 should not set verdicts directly. Order of work, each a child bead filed from this spike:

1. **Decide the budget verdict rule and extend the schema** (blocks the rest; done in `hemsida-dl5.6`, the rule as decided is in `docs/data-model.md`, the text below is the original proposal, superseded on the nominal-only and opposition points): a budget verdict is allowed from a `budget` evidence entry with an explicit tier. `fulfilled` requires an anchoring `prop`/`bet` plus outcome in the promised direction in real terms for the final outcome year; `partially_fulfilled` when only the nominal figure moves as promised; `in_progress` stays as today (prop evidence). `unfulfilled`/`broken` stay term-close/explicit-act only. Stated intention (`figures`) still never qualifies. Add `tier` (`ceiling`|`outcome`) and an optional `baselineNote` to the evidence, update `checkVerdictEvidence`, `docs/data-model.md` and its tests, and decide how opposition promises are measured.
2. **Settle the 46 `anslag` promises** in batches by area (transport, rättsväsende, energy, rest), one PR each, after step 1.
3. **Handle the 7 `anslag_macro` promises**: decide the definition (NATO figure vs UO 6, ODA vs UO 7), fetch BNP 2025 when SCB publishes it (macro.json ends 2024), then compute.
4. **Extract stated-intention figures for the 77 `prop_text` promises** from their linked prop/bet texts into `figures[]`. This adds timeline content but no verdict, except that existing `in_progress` rules still apply.
5. **Re-type the 23 `rate_law` promises** from `budget` to `legislative` and run them through the candidate/judge/SFS flow of dl5.1.
6. **Decide the 17 `not_answerable` promises** (`not_assessable` with reasoning, or hold for the outcome epic `hemsida-50r`).

Beads: 1 = `hemsida-dl5.6`, 2 = `hemsida-dl5.7` (blocked by dl5.6), 3 = `hemsida-dl5.8`, 4 = `hemsida-dl5.9`, 5 = `hemsida-dl5.10`, 6 = `hemsida-dl5.11`.
