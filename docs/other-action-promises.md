# Other-action promises: what can be checked?

Findings of `hemsida-dl5.3`, 2026-11-23. Scope: the 294 unassessed `type: other_action` promises of 2022-2026 (25 Tidö, 269 val2022). The machine-readable classification with a one-line reason per promise is `docs/other-action-classification.json`.

## What the rules allow

- `checkVerdictEvidence` (`src/schema/promise.ts`) lets `in_progress` stand on a `dir`, `sou`, `ds` or `prop` entry, and `fulfilled` / `partially_fulfilled` only on an `sfs` entry (a `dir` for inquiry promises). A `bet` or a skrivelse qualifies nothing but `broken`.
- `apply-links` never gives an `other_action` promise `fulfilled` or `partially_fulfilled` (no law step, `docs/data-model.md`), and it skips skrivelser as evidence. So the most a Riksdag document can do for such a promise today is `in_progress`.
- `no_evidence` is reserved for an exhaustive search over a fully ingested term (`docs/data-model.md`), so it is not set here.
- Most of what these promises ask for (regeringsuppdrag, agency instructions, förordningar, EU or UN positions, party pledges such as "no cooperation with SD") is decided by the government and leaves no Riksdag document. The tracker has no source for regeringsbeslut or förordningar, so those cannot be checked.

## Result of the class-a run

Link run `link-other-action-sonnet-v1-dl5-3` (Sonnet through the local login, 144 pairs, up to 10 candidates per promise, about $2.5 of subscription usage). Every proposed pair was read against the promise and the document.

- **`tido-nationell-social-insatsstyrka`: `unassessed` -> `in_progress`.** Ds 2025:19 is the final report of the assignment on förstärkningsteam inom socialtjänsten; SOU 2024:43 says that this is what Tidöavtalet calls the social insatsstyrka. The one Ds is the evidence, `partial`.
- **Off topic, rejected** (`rejected-candidates.json`): SOU HAB367 for the same promise (a knowledge organisation that explicitly does no operational support), Dir. 2026:34 for `val2022-m-renodla-polisens-uppdrag` (Säkerhetspolisen only), Ds HBB418 for `val2022-mp-dorr-ut-for-valdsutsatta` (tenancy law only), dir HBB1161 for the Nato promise (export-control rules that presuppose membership), prop HB03134 for the SAS promise (SAS restructuring, not a sale).
- **Held back on purpose: `val2022-s-sverige-ska-ga-med-i-nato`.** The judge found the on-topic proposition (prop. 2022/23:74) and Ds 2022:24, and the Riksdag approved the accession in 2023. `in_progress` ("Pågår") would be wrong for a promise that has been delivered, and the rules cannot express `fulfilled` for a treaty accession (no SFS law is the deliverable).
