## Conservative calls on uncertain items

The proposal marked 63 items "Osäker". They were re-read against the manifesto text and the linked Tidö promises; the proposed label was kept where the evidence supports it. These are the ones where the evidence did not settle it and the more conservative label was chosen, listed for a later human check:

- `stopp-religiosa-friskolor`: Tidö only tightens inspection of confessional schools; no ban. not_addressed rather than weakened.
- `enklare-omplacering-av-stormande-elever`: Tidö only commits to inquire into legislation. weakened rather than included.

- `stoppa-kompetensutvisningarna`: Tidö p37 protects existing practice but does not promise to stop the deportations. not_addressed rather than weakened.
- `asylratten-varnas-den-med-skal-ska-stanna`: Temporary protection could be read as contradicting; only the return half matches. weakened rather than included.
- `nytt-brott-tjanstemannaforfoljelse`: No new offence, only higher penalties for threats against officials. weakened rather than included.

- `utvisning-ska-alltid-provas-vid-grova-brott`: Tidö covers all crimes, a superset of L:s; kept included (not strengthened) because the label should not imply a judgement about scope.
- `utreda-vistelseforbud-domda-kriminella`: Tidö introduces a residence ban in geographic areas, not limited to vulnerable areas, instead of only trying the possibility; kept included.
